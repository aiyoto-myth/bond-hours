How it works

Where does our families' information go?

Nowhere. Everything you paste stays in this browser on this computer. There is no account and no server copy. Use "Download backup" below to move it to another computer or keep a copy.

How does it match a shift to a family?

By email, then by a parent's or player's full name. If the email says one family and the name says another, it asks. Anything else goes in the yellow "Needs you" box: you pick the family, and full names are remembered for the next paste (see "Remembered names" under the log to undo one).

Two parents signed up for the same shift. Does it count twice?

Your choice in step 1. Many policies count it once per family ("hours are counted for the event, not for each volunteer"). The same shift pasted twice from two sheets is also counted once.

Coaches and managers are exempt. Assistant coaches get 6 hours. How?

Under "roles that cover the bond" in step 1. A role can cover the whole bond, give a number of hours, or mark a family that paid the buyout (no cheque held). Put the role in the family list or pick it on the family's line.

Can team managers use it during the season?

Yes. Free covers a team. Add shifts after each game, then use "Download shifts CSV" under the log and send that file to the coordinator, who pastes it into step 3.

A shift was matched by surname only. Is it counted?

Not until you say so. "The Smiths" or "J. Garcia" shows up in the yellow box with the family it probably is; one click accepts it. Full names and emails are matched on their own.

Know the volunteer coordinator at another association? Send them the tool. It works the same for them, and their families' names stay on their computer.
